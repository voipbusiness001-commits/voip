What Is a VoIP Phone Number?

A VOIP phone number is a telephone number that allows users to make and receive calls over the internet instead of traditional telephone lines. VoIP stands for Voice over Internet Protocol, a technology that converts voice into digital data and transmits it through an internet connection.

Businesses use VoIP phone numbers to reduce communication costs, manage customer calls, and stay connected from different locations. These Phone Number solutions can work on smartphones, computers, and compatible desk phones, making them ideal for remote teams and growing businesses.

Unlike traditional landlines, VoIP phone numbers often include features such as call forwarding, voicemail, call recording, and conference calling. For businesses looking for a flexible and cost-effective communication solution, a VoIP business phone number can be a practical choice.
